NOTES
  The letters are drawn as vector paths, not type, so no font is needed
  and the shapes cannot shift on a machine that lacks the typeface.

  The 16px icon does not really work: the three racket shapes merge.
  Before shipping anything that small, have a reduced mark drawn — a
  single racket head, or a stringbed grid.

  The one-colour files use an outlined square rather than a knockout.
  If your printer wants a true knockout they can make one from the SVG.
